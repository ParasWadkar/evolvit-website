What happens next

  1. You apply This form, plus the team you want to work with.
  2. Short conversation A quick chat with the team leads about what you want to build.
  3. You're in You join a team and start on the next event cycle.

What we look for

  • People who finish what they start — deadlines here are real.
  • Curiosity over credentials. First-years are welcome and encouraged.
  • Something you've made, however small — a project, poster, reel or article.
  • Willingness to work across teams when an event needs it.
