EvolVIT — Club Mission

EvolVIT exists for one reason: the distance between a syllabus and a sprint board is bigger than most students realise. We close it with industrial visits, hackathons, seminars, and structured interview and placement prep — giving VIT Bhopal students a direct line to real industry problems, and the credibility to solve them.

We don't stop at exposure. EvolVIT partners directly with companies to place selected members into real internships — not simulated case studies, but live products with real deadlines and real users. Bridging classrooms to corporate, building futures, together.

Student-led Open to all branches 6 core teams Industry-backed

Multi-stage builds and pitch rounds on real problem statements — judged on innovation, feasibility, technical viability and scalability, not just slides.

Getting in

How the Research Team gets built.

Top-performing club members earn a shot at the Research Team through a structured, five-stage evaluation — built to keep the credibility of our industry partnerships high.

01

Initial Screening

Interested participants submit applications, reviewed by the team to shortlist suitable candidates.

02

Skill Assessment

Shortlisted candidates get individual mini-projects on a defined timeline, testing real technical competence.

03

Performance Evaluation

Candidates are evaluated on how they actually performed in their assigned mini-projects.

04

Company Assessment

Evaluated candidates sit an additional assessment or test conducted directly by the hiring company.

05

Internship Placement

Candidates who clear the company's assessment move straight into the internship.

Behind the scenes

The six teams behind every EvolVIT event.

From first concept to final wrap-up, every event runs on six core teams working in sync. Here's what each one owns — and where you'd fit in.

Tech Team

The technical backbone of every event — submission portals, judging platforms, on-ground tech support — and the team that drives hackathon and challenge design.

Content Team

Writes the words behind every event — problem statements, scripts, certificates, reports — keeping the club's message clear and consistent.

Event Management

Runs the logistics — venue booking, scheduling, on-day coordination, volunteers — keeping every event on track from registration to close.

Design Team

Builds the visual identity for every event — posters, banners, certificates, decks, digital creatives — turning concepts into visuals that catch attention first.

Social Media & Photography

Manages EvolVIT's online presence and documents every event live — photos, reels, recaps — building the club's public story.

PR & Outreach

Builds relationships with companies, guest speakers, and partner clubs — securing collaborations and handling internal and external communication.

What we've run so far.

Four flagship events in our first tenure as the 100th club of VIT Bhopal University — from a launch exhibition to an industrial visit that members earned by competing for it.

Event 01 · Inaugural Event · Poster Exhibition

EvolART

EvolVIT's very first event — a poster exhibition and competition marking our launch as the 100th club of VIT Bhopal University. A multi-stage contest, it pushed students to explore the shift from human to artificial intelligence, digital detox, and student mental stress through creative visual storytelling.

Round 1 (online submission) drew 81 solo, duo and trio teams, judged by a five-member EvolVIT panel on relevance, creativity, detailing and visual impact. The top 15 teams advanced to a live poster exhibition at the Mini Auditorium — judged in the presence of Vice Chancellor Dr. T. B. Shridharan, the DSW, and the Assistant DSW.

Event 02 · Ideathon · Fully Online

Idea2Industry

Idea2Industry challenged students to build technically feasible solutions to real-world problem statements — sharpening analytical thinking and pushing participants to design industry-relevant answers that actually bridge theory and implementation, while building teamwork, creativity, and an entrepreneurial mindset.

Run entirely online across multiple stages, participants presented their problem analysis, proposed solution, feasibility and industry impact before a panel of judges. Entries were scored on innovation, originality, practicality, technical viability, scalability and clarity of presentation.

Event 03 · Industrial Visit

Appointy, Bhopal

EvolVIT organised an industrial visit to Appointy, Bhopal, giving 40 students immersive exposure to a real technology-driven organisation — the top 30 performers from Idea2Industry among them, having earned the trip as that event's selection criteria. Students were introduced to the company's journey, vision and global presence — including how a Bhopal-based company builds scalable solutions for international clients.

They got a detailed look at the product development lifecycle — planning, coding, testing, deployment, continuous improvement — with emphasis on structured problem-solving, QA and user-centric design. A highlight was the discussion on AI-driven transformation: automation, data-driven decisions, and the skills needed to stay competitive. An open Q&A let students get career guidance directly from industry professionals.

Event 04 · Orientation · With Defence Warrior Club

Youth as Game Changers for Viksit Bharat

Themed “Role from 2026 to 2047,” this orientation was organised jointly with the Defence Warrior Club to sensitise students to their role in building a Developed India by 2047. Chief Guest Brigadier Rajneesh Gaur, Deputy Director General of the NCC MP & CG Directorate, addressed the gathering on discipline, leadership and national service.

The Brigadier spoke on discipline, dedication and leadership in achieving personal and national goals, and on how the NCC shapes character in young people. Questions

Before you apply

Do I need to already know how to code?

No. EvolVIT runs six different teams — tech, content, event management, design, social media and photography, and PR and outreach — so there is real work whatever your strength. If you do want to go technical, our events and workshops are built to take you from curious to competent.

Which branches and years can join?

All of them. We're a university-wide club, and our events are open to every branch and year at VIT Bhopal. Teams are assembled on interest and effort, not on your CGPA or your department.

How do the internships actually work?

Our Research Team closes collaborations with companies, then top-performing club members go through a five-stage selection — screening, a mini-project skill assessment, performance evaluation, an assessment run by the hiring company itself, and finally placement. Past placements include a 3-month internship with Datatrack, Bhopal (counted for 4 college credits) and a 1-month full-stack sprint with Quantumard.

What kind of projects will I work on?

Live ones. Members have shipped production work including Uprole, an AI Worksheet Analyzer, Atithi (a visitor management system), a centralized EV discovery platform, and a full-stack project management system with role-based access built on Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind and a Node/Express/MongoDB backend.

What's the time commitment?

It scales with what you take on. Attending workshops, seminars and hackathons is light. Owning an event workstream or joining an internship cohort means real deadlines — which is exactly the point, because that's the part that translates to a job floor.
